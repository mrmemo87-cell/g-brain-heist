# 🎓 Sign Up Guide for Students

Welcome to **Brains Heist**! Follow these easy steps to create your account and start your learning adventure.

---

## **Step 1: Go to the Sign-Up Page**
- Visit the Brains Heist website
- Click on the **"Sign Up"** button (or tab)

---

## **Step 2: Enter Your Basic Information**

### You need to provide:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Username** | Your "codename" - how other agents will know you | `StealthNinja` or `CodeBreaker` |
| **Email** | Your email address (you'll need to verify it later) | `student@email.com` |
| **Password** | A secure password (use a mix of letters & numbers) | `MySecure123!` |

**💡 Tips:**
- Choose a username that's at least 3 characters
- Make your password strong and something you'll remember
- Use a real email address (you'll get a verification email)

---

## **Step 3: Complete Your Profile**

After signing up, you'll see a **Setup Wizard** that asks for more details:

### What you'll need to select:

| Information | Your Choice |
|------------|-------------|
| **Your Role** | Select "Student" |
| **Your Grade** | Pick your grade level (6-12) |
| **Your Class/Batch** | Choose your class (e.g., 6A, 6B) |
| **Your School** | Select your school from the dropdown |

**✅ Complete all fields before continuing**

---

## **Step 4: Verify Your Email**

1. Check your **email inbox** (including spam/junk folder)
2. Look for an email from Brains Heist
3. Click the **verification link** in the email
4. Your account is now verified! ✨

---

## **Step 5: Start Learning!**

🎉 **You're all set!**

- Log in with your email and password
- Explore quizzes and challenges
- Join your class and compete with friends
- Earn gems and level up! 💎

---

## **Common Questions**

### **"I didn't get a verification email"**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Wait a few minutes and refresh your email
- Contact your teacher if you still don't see it

### **"I forgot my password"**
- Click **"Forgot password?"** on the login page
- Enter your email
- Follow the reset instructions sent to your email

### **"Can I change my username later?"**
- Ask your teacher or administrator for help
- Contact the support team

### **"What should my password be?"**
- At least 8 characters long
- Mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
- Don't use easy words like "password123"
- Example: `Science2024!`

---

## **Need Help?**

- 📧 Contact your teacher
- 💬 Ask your classmates
- 🆘 Reach out to the support team
