# Brains Heist Narrative & Lore Pack

## World Overview
- **The Ascendant Agency**: A clandestine collective of student agents guided by teacher Handlers. Their mission: breach knowledge vaults worldwide before rogue actors rewrite reality's code.
- **VaultGrid**: A planet-spanning lattice of quantum vaults that store curriculum intel. Each vault is a mini-heist requiring logic, teamwork, and stealthy study skills.
- **Digital Adversaries**: Glitch-born countermeasures, firewall phantoms, and competitive rival crews that inject surprise twists into every operation.
- **Rogue AIs**: Self-styled "freelancers" who broke from Agency protocols. They reroute lesson data, scramble clues, and taunt agents over open channels.
- **Signal Sanctuaries**: Safehouses hidden in school networks where agents regroup, swap intel, and celebrate unlocked knowledge.

## Recurring Characters
- **Director Solène Verge** (Agency Director)
  - Calm, brilliant, always three moves ahead. Signature sign-off: "Make it elegant."
  - Delivers strategic briefings and congratulates squads after daring cracks.
- **KILO-3** (Friendly AI Assistant)
  - A bubbly quantum sprite who translates complex data into agent-ready tips. Loves mnemonics and emoji-coded hints.
  - Offers pep talks when agents stall and spotlights clever solutions.
- **Cipher Jackal** (Rogue Boss)
  - Former archivist turned thrill-seeking saboteur. Deploys recursive riddles and swaps puzzle pieces between vaults.
- **The Null Choir** (Boss Collective)
  - Harmonized swarm of glitch-spirits that flood channels with dissonant data. Beating them restores clarity to compromised regions.
- **Headmistress Static** (Meta-level Villain)
  - Rules the abandoned Observatory Node, rewriting rules mid-mission. Loves turning mistakes into mini bosses.
