## Problem and correction

The original pack named L-003/L-004 without explaining that Audio-player.html plays those same MP3 files. The teacher plan did not name exact files or slide cues at every step, and the workbook's Reading directions could be mistaken for a missing paper activity. The Reading transition also needed to distinguish sign-in/preparation from starting an independent attempt.

The complete ZIP now opens with `01-START-HERE.html`, a linked file map and seven timed steps. Each step names the file/slide, teacher actions, suggested speech and a checkpoint before moving on. The separate teacher plan uses the same runbook. The online lesson overview derives from those same steps. Both the online download controls and TXT README point to the guide first.

The guide explains the exact sequence: workbook L-003 with slide 3 and Round 1 audio; committed answers followed by slides 4–16; workbook L-004 with slide 17 and Round 2 audio; review through slide 30; goals/sign-in and the Reading entry page; independent online Reading; exit/follow-up. No answer reveal occurs during either recording. The audio player labels round/code/title, pauses the other recording on play, reports completion/error, and links back to the guide.

Reading remains the actual governed `/ielts/reading-screener` route. The workbook explicitly contains directions only and needs no lesson audio. The entry page's Start Reading screener / Resume screener / View saved result labels were checked in the current implementation. The guide does not force completed students to repeat. Ready students remain on the entry page during preparation and start in the Reading block. If the published duration, devices or access cannot fit, the teacher schedules a separate full session before starting rather than shortening the assessment or marking unfinished work complete. For Plan B, the pack separately includes a released R-001 paper Reading practice sheet, private key and timed teacher guide. This is explicitly not a printed copy of Reading Screener A and creates no submitted online result. The guide distinguishes failure before start, interrupted active attempts and partial-class outages; it protects existing work, delays answer-key discussion while assessments remain active, and records support/exposure. If R-001 is withdrawn or unavailable, the paper downloads are disabled and the Plan B guide supplies a quiet workbook fallback without broken paper links.
