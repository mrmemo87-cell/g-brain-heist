# Advanced continuation after Lesson 24

Version 0.1.0 · 2026-10-17 · Private planning draft. Diagnostic Bible 1.10.0; Lessons Bible 1.1.0; Visual & UX Bible 1.1.0.

The 24-lesson reference course starts at B1 and builds test control: question types, timing, task structure and evidence-based review. Learners who aim for a high Academic band (7.5 to 8.5) need more than that. This document plans what comes after Lesson 24. It is a plan, not a promise: no sequence of lessons guarantees a band, and only the official test produces one.

## Who continues

Learners continue to the advanced route only when their saved evidence supports it, not by self-selection or calendar. As a planning guide, the teacher looks for all of the following in fresh, timed work of full test demand:

- Listening and Reading: fresh full sections completed in time, with remaining errors concentrated in a small number of identifiable traps (for example Not Given reasoning or plan labelling) rather than spread across all question types.
- Writing: complete Task 1 and Task 2 responses written in time, on topic, with a clear position and paragraphing, where the remaining comments concern precision, range and development rather than task completion.
- Speaking: full three-part interviews observed live in which the learner sustains Part 2 for close to two minutes and develops Part 3 answers without prompting.

If the evidence is missing or mixed, the learner repeats targeted cycles from Lessons 17–23 first. That decision is recorded with its evidence, not as a level label.

## What changes at the advanced stage

| Skill | Course focus (Lessons 1–24) | Advanced focus |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | Distractors, spelling, plan labelling, timed sections | Part 4 lectures at natural speed with dense academic vocabulary; accents beyond the course voice set; full four-part tests under exam timing; zero-tolerance spelling and number checks |
| Reading | Locating evidence, TFNG/YNNG, headings, timing one passage | Three passages in 60 minutes; longer argumentative texts; writer's view across paragraphs; summary completion from a word list; consistent accuracy on the last passage |
| Writing | Complete, organised Task 1 and Task 2 responses | Precise overviews of complex visuals (mixed charts, processes, maps); nuanced positions with concession; lexical precision and collocation; error-free complex sentences; editing to remove repetition |
| Speaking | Developed answers in all three parts | Abstract Part 3 discussion with speculation and hypothesis; idiomatic but accurate language; self-correction; pronunciation features observed live (chunking, stress, intonation) |

## Proposed advanced cycle

Each two-week cycle has four 60-minute lessons plus one separately scheduled timed block.

1. Diagnose: one fresh full-length section or task under test conditions, outside the lesson.
2. Teach: one lesson on the highest-frequency error found, with original high-demand material.
3. Apply: one lesson of timed practice at full test demand, without support.
4. Productive clinic: one Writing or Speaking lesson using criterion comments that quote the learner's work.
5. Review: one lesson comparing only evidence of similar demand and conditions, and agreeing the next cycle's focus.

Six cycles (twelve weeks) form the reference advanced block. As in the main course, the calendar adapts to the evidence and the school timetable.

## Evidence rules (unchanged from the course)

- Only saved platform results, teacher-confirmed reviews, dated live observations and fresh checks with recorded conditions count as evidence.
- Practice, corrected and repeated work stays labelled as practice.
- No band, percentage, readiness label or certificate of level is produced by classroom material. A full timed practice test under recorded conditions is still practice and is reported as such.
- Writing and Speaking judgements use the public band descriptors only through calibrated teacher review; peer comments are practice notes.

## Production gates before any advanced lesson is used

- Lessons Bible contracts LB-01 to LB-11 apply to every advanced pack, built with the same [lesson factory](LESSON_FACTORY.md) and source contract.
- Full-length Listening recordings need the same audio production record and owner listening approval as Lessons 5, 10, 14, 17 and 21. Part 4 lectures need a single-speaker academic voice and natural pacing.
- High-demand Reading passages need originality, difficulty and item review by a qualified human reviewer before use.
- Writing models for this stage remain illustrative and labelled as such; they are never presented as certified band answers.
- Teacher calibration on the public Writing and Speaking descriptors is recorded before advanced clinics run.
