## Teacher and student use

Teachers open IELTS Programme → Assign practice, select a published task, search or page through their school's eligible students, inspect the matching source evidence and assign with a reason. The selector does not automatically choose Gulzada or assign tasks to every student. Writing/Speaking sources still require the corresponding teacher review. Students open their assigned task in IELTS Journey → Targeted Practice. These targeted checks are not a freely browsable answer-bearing catalogue; independent checks remain protected from prior exposure.

School permissions, active membership, IELTS entitlement, banned-account denial, exact source ownership, concealed keys/transcripts, signed audio, repeat/exposure checks and teacher feedback controls remain. Unreleased future tasks retain their named-student pilot. Practice does not establish an IELTS band or improvement by itself.
