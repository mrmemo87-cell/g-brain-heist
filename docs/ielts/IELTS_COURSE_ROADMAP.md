## Intake and first-month operations

Before Lesson 2, check the four screeners and productive-skill review states in authorized Student progress/Review desk. Do not inspect questions on a projector. For each learner record joining date, actual source/review dates, up to two supported priorities, one next action and a check date. Arrange unfinished screeners outside the lesson with their full published duration. Do not replace them with the classroom tasks in this pack.

Shared lessons continue while new learners complete intake. Catch-up covers the missed teaching essentials, not a retake of completed assessments. Foundation support is optional and recorded. Extension increases response demand without assigning a fixed ability label.

Silk Road first review target: 6 November 2026, Asia/Bishkek. This is a school-specific operational date, not a hardcoded reusable lesson default. Use each learner's actual joining date and an explicit evidence cutoff; flag interim/partial periods. Later review dates depend on the real school calendar and holidays. The programme's 12 teaching weeks need not be 12 uninterrupted calendar weeks.
