# Academic Profile evidence coverage

The reported Grade 7 ESL profile showed zero completed assignments while its
collective classroom report showed 14/30 correct (47%). The database had retained
all 30 qualified answer records. The profile compared raw subject strings:
the assignment and observations used English, while the selected school subject
was ESL. The same student queried through English already returned 46.7%.

Profile filtering, teacher subject authorization, subject summaries and activity
labels now use the school's governed academic subject mappings and aliases.
Class and collective-group labels remain presentation and allocation context;
they do not become separate academic subject identities. Assignment membership,
marks, question hashes, observation history and stable skill keys are unchanged.
Unmapped labels retain their exact identity rather than broadening to another subject.

Official assignment summaries also accept immutable registry item evidence,
including the newer Economics pipeline, alongside the existing curriculum ledger.
Only graded answers with intact verified snapshots, eligible grades, matching
school/year/subject context and independent evidence qualify. Targeted practice
and private teacher questions remain excluded. Screen and printable trend charts
recognize registry assignments as assignment evidence.

Writing Hub's final teacher review requirement remains in place. Pending
assessments are disclosed separately and do not enter the assignment average.
At audit time Silk Road had 24 assessments, all awaiting final review. A
rollback-only finalization check generated four qualified rubric observations
and exposed them through the ESL profile without creating assignment outcomes.
No test review or fabricated student result was retained.

Live current-year coverage checks matched 22 qualified assignment outcomes across
19 student/subject checks with no mismatches. The reported student's ESL profile
was verified after migration using the authenticated teacher role: one completed
assignment, 46.7% and 30 evidence records. This is not proof of established mastery:
longitudinal conclusions retain their existing evidence sufficiency gates.

Regression tests execute the actual SQL subject helpers and registry summary view
in PGlite, including wrong-school, pending-grade, changed-content, private-pool
and targeted-practice rejection. Existing profile, writing and registry tests,
typecheck, migration security guard and production build are also checked.
