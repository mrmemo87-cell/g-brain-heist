# Academic profile: assessment grouping and teacher readability

Answers from a single verified diagnostic previously counted as separate skill assessments. Mixed correct and incorrect answers could trigger a contradictory state, and the chart spread different skills over a single date. Latest skill results could depend on which answer was last in the observation list.

The database now pools qualified assignment observations within each student, skill, academic year and source assessment. Item counts still contribute evidence volume, while assessment counts contribute independent observations. The existing confidence and longitudinal eligibility thresholds remain in force. Conflict detection requires separate assessment dates. Current-year affected derived summaries are rebuilt; raw observations and archived evidence remain intact. Private helper permissions and public RPC authorization remain closed to unauthorised callers.

Screen and print share a question-weighted assessment model. Correct/total counts take precedence over rounded percentages. Single-date evidence uses a skill-result table, while comparable progress remains separated by source and tested focus coverage. The first section shows the assignment result, question total, evidence stage and suggested follow-up. Initial positive results remain distinct from established strengths. Detailed evidence expands by assessment and skill to reveal existing qualified question statements or writing corrections. Final Writing Hub reviews remain separate from assignment averages, with pending reviews explicitly shown.

Validation: full repository verification, focused assessment aggregation tests and actual SQL helper execution under PGlite; production-data checks performed within rolled-back transactions. The example diagnostic resolves to one assessment, 30 question items across 16 skills, with no false conflict flags. Teacher-filtered ESL continues to resolve to canonical English and the official assignment result remains 46.7% (14/30, rounded to 47% in the overview).
