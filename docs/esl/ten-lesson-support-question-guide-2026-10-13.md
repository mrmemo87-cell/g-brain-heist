## What is available

Both pathways cover the same ten lesson themes; Grade 7 uses shorter sentences and texts, while Grade 8 uses slightly longer contexts, familiar everyday situations, and controlled additional complexity. These labels describe task design, not a formally measured CEFR level.

## Use in class

1. Teach and practise with separate examples. Review the chosen items before assigning them.
2. Give the six lesson-check questions individually, without hints, partner answers or teacher correction during the check.
3. Keep the four fresh-recheck questions unseen. Assess them about one week later in a separate completed assignment so Academic Profile receives a later dated observation, not a duplicate result.
4. Use the verified sets online or through confirmed paper answers. Printing alone generates no evidence. Unfinished work is not a completed assessment.
5. At fewer than 5/6 on the initial check or 3/4 on the recheck, revisit the specific target and use a shorter task before increasing difficulty. These are classroom planning thresholds, not changes to the platform's confidence or mastery rules.
6. One six-question check is limited evidence. Do not declare mastery from one score or treat several questions about the same passage as several independent assessment occasions.

## Prerequisites for Lessons 9–10

Lesson 9 is an extension until the learner can reliably select have + participle. Lesson 10 is an extension until the learner can use irregular participles and had + participle. If prerequisites are not secure, use that teaching slot to reteach the verb system and reassess before introducing modal-perfect or third-conditional structures. Grade 7's third-conditional result check was 1/14; Grade 8's third-conditional items were 8/26. Those results do not justify immediately assigning full advanced conditional work to everyone.

Past-continuous items were stronger at 23/26. The Grade 8 diagnostic did not establish reading performance, so Lessons 3–4 provide an initial baseline rather than claiming a demonstrated Grade 8 reading deficit.
- The latest irregular-verbs assignment had only one completion at the planning check; it was not used to claim a whole-class weakness.

## Academic mapping and validation

Every item has one approved registry mapping, a clear item-specific evidence statement and a matching assessment process. Existing evidence focuses were reused for 102 items where the target matched earlier evidence. New focuses cover the remaining controlled tasks. All 200 items are active, globally public, verified and analytics-eligible with matching content hashes. Keys occur 50 times each in positions A–D. Importer dry runs passed before publication. Live verification confirmed ten items per topic, six check and four recheck items, and exact intervention candidates for all 23 Grade 7 and 24 Grade 8 targets tested with representative enrolled student contexts.

MCQs provide evidence of recognition, form selection, reading retrieval and inference; they do not establish independent speaking or extended-writing competence. Student scores and old evidence snapshots were not changed. Actual improvement requires new completed work showing stronger performance.
