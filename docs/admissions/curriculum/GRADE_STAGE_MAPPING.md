# Admission Grade-to-Stage Mapping

Grade and curriculum level/stage are separate fields. A curriculum map is valid only when the map includes an explicit mapping decision. Cambridge-linked maps use Cambridge stages; Brain Heist International maps may use general admission-readiness level definitions.

| School grade | Cambridge-linked default | Brain Heist International default |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Cambridge Primary | 1 |
| 2 | Cambridge Primary | 2 |
| 3 | Cambridge Primary | 3 |
| 4 | Cambridge Primary | 4 |
| 5 | Cambridge Primary | 5 |
| 6 | Cambridge Primary | 6 |
| 7 | Cambridge Lower Secondary | 7 |
| 8 | Cambridge Lower Secondary | 8 |
| 9 | Cambridge Lower Secondary | 9 |
| 10 | Cambridge IGCSE exact selected syllabus when explicitly used, not generic Stage 10 | General international Grade 10 admission-readiness level with prerequisites |

Any exception must be documented in map metadata with `grade_stage_mapping.explicit: true`, rationale, reviewer, and date.
