# 🎮 Student Quick Start Guide

Welcome to **G-Brains Heist**! You're about to become a cyber agent in a neon digital world.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started (2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Open the Game
Visit the URL your teacher gave you. It looks something like:
- `https://g-brain-heist.netlify.app` or
- `http://192.168.1.100:3000` (classroom network)

### Step 2: "Login"
- Enter ANY email and password
- It's just for fun - no real account needed!
- Click "Initialize Heist Sequence"

### Step 3: You're In!
You'll see your dashboard with:
- Your profile (NeonGhost, Level 12)
- 8,750 coins to start
- Tasks to complete
- News feed showing activities

---

## 🎯 What To Do First

### 1️⃣ **Complete a Quest** (Earn XP & Coins)
- Click the blue **"Start Quest"** button
- Pick a subject (Cryptography, Network Security, etc.)
- Answer 5 multiple choice questions
- Get rewards for correct answers!

### 2️⃣ **Check Your Tasks** (Daily Objectives)
- Look at the "Active Missions" card
- Complete them for bonus rewards
- Daily tasks reset every 24 hours

### 3️⃣ **Visit the Shop** (Buy Power-Ups)
- Click the purple **"Visit Shop"** button
- Buy shields to protect your coins
- Get boosters for extra XP
- Use your coins wisely!

### 4️⃣ **Join or Create a Clan** (Team Up!)
- Click **"Clans"** button
- Join an existing clan OR
- Create your own (costs 1,000 coins)
- Chat with clan members
- Donate to clan vault for team buffs

### 5️⃣ **PvP Hack Rivals** (Challenge Others)
- Click **"Hack Rival"** when you have AP (Action Points)
- Choose a target carefully
- Win = steal coins!
- Lose = lose XP
- Shields protect you from attacks

---

## 📊 Understanding Your Stats

### 💰 **Coins**
- Earned from quests and PvP wins
- Spend in shop for items
- Donate to your clan

### ⚡ **XP (Experience Points)**
- Gain from completing quests
- Level up when you get enough
- Lost if you fail PvP attacks

### 🔋 **AP (Action Points)**
- Used for PvP attacks
- Regenerates over time
- Max 20 AP

### 🔥 **Streak**
- Days you've played in a row
- Higher streak = bonus rewards
- Don't break the chain!

### 📈 **Level**
- Overall progress indicator
- Unlocks new features
- Shows on leaderboard

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Getting Rich Fast
1. 💊 **Use boosters BEFORE quests** for 1.5x - 2x XP
2. 🎯 **Complete daily tasks** - easy rewards
3. ⚔️ **Attack weaker players** - higher win chance
4. 🛡️ **Always have a shield active** - protection

### Strategy
- **Morning routine:** Check tasks, buy shield, do quests
- **Save coins:** Don't waste on cosmetics early
- **Join active clan:** Teamwork makes the dream work
- **Target wisely in PvP:** Check opponent's shield status

### Avoiding Mistakes
- ❌ Don't attack with shields unless you have a cracker
- ❌ Don't ignore daily tasks - they're easy XP
- ❌ Don't spam PvP - you need AP to recover
- ❌ Don't forget to activate items after buying

## 🎮 Game Modes Explained

### 📚 Knowledge Quests
**What:** Answer multiple choice questions  
**Rewards:** XP + Coins per correct answer  
**Best For:** Leveling up, learning  
**Tip:** Use boosters first for max gains

### ⚔️ PvP Hacks
**What:** Attack other players  
**Rewards:** Steal coins if you win  
**Risk:** Lose XP if you fail  
**Best For:** Getting rich, competition  
**Tip:** Check win % before attacking

### 🛒 Shop
**What:** Buy power-ups and cosmetics  
**Items:**
- **Shield** (150 coins): Blocks 1 attack
- **Cracker** (200 coins): Bypass enemy shields
- **Booster** (250 coins): 1.5x XP for 1 hour
- **Major Booster** (400 coins): 2x XP for 1 hour
- **Cosmetics** (750+ coins): Look cool

### 👥 Clans
**What:** Team up with other players  
**Benefits:**
- Chat with members
- Share resources
- Unlock team buffs
- Collaborate on goals

**Roles:**
- **Leader:** Full control, can kick/promote
- **Officer:** Can manage some settings
- **Member:** Basic privileges

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "I lost all my progress!"
**Solution:** Don't clear browser data! Progress is saved in your browser.  
**Prevention:** Use the same browser and device each time.

### "I can't attack anyone"
**Problem:** You're out of AP (Action Points)  
**Solution:** Wait for AP to regenerate, or come back later.

### "I have no coins"
**Solution:**
1. Complete knowledge quests (guaranteed coins)
2. Finish daily tasks
3. Don't attack targets with shields unless you have crackers

### "Game won't load"
**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page (F5)
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
4. Tell your teacher
