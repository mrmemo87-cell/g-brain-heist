Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

Summary: You can cancel anytime — access continues until the end of the billing period. Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase if you haven't substantially used the service. All billing is handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

1. Cancellation

1.1 How to Cancel

School administrators can cancel their subscription at any time through:

  • In-app: School Admin Portal → Billing → Manage Subscription
  • Paddle portal: Use the management link provided in your subscription confirmation email
  • Email: Send a cancellation request to support@brainsheist.com

1.2 What Happens When You Cancel

  • Your subscription will not renew at the end of the current billing period.
  • You retain full access to all paid features until the end of the period you've already paid for.
  • After the billing period ends, your school reverts to the free tier (Lockdown mode only).
  • Student data, progress, and scores are preserved — nothing is deleted. If you resubscribe later, everything is restored.

⚠️ No partial-period refunds for cancellations. If you cancel mid-period, you keep access until the period ends, but we do not refund the unused portion. This is because Paddle processes billing on our behalf and subscription charges are for the full period.

2. Refunds

2.1 14-Day Refund Window

We offer a 14-day refund from the date of your initial subscription purchase, subject to the following conditions:

ConditionRefund Eligible?
Within 14 days of first purchase, minimal usage✅ Full refund
Within 14 days, but substantial usage (e.g., 50+ student logins, 10+ assessments)⚠️ Partial refund at our discretion
After 14 days❌ No refund (cancel to stop future charges)
Auto-renewal charge (monthly/yearly)✅ Full refund if requested within 48 hours of renewal
Plan upgrade (mid-cycle)✅ Prorated credit applied automatically by Paddle
Free pilot trialN/A — no charge was made

2.2 How to Request a Refund

  1. Email support@brainsheist.com with subject line: "Refund Request — [School Name]"
  2. Include your school name, the email used for purchase, and reason for the request.
  3. We will review and respond within 2 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are processed by Paddle and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.

2.3 Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Paddle. We cannot issue refunds to a different payment method or as account credit.

3. Plan Changes

3.1 Upgrades

  • You can upgrade your plan at any time.
  • Paddle prorates the cost — you pay only the difference for the remaining billing period.
  • The upgraded plan takes effect immediately.

3.2 Downgrades

  • Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.
  • You retain your current plan's features until the period ends.
  • If your school has more active users than the lower plan allows, you may need to adjust membership before downgrading.

4. Failed Payments

  • If a payment fails, Paddle will retry the charge automatically over several days.
  • During this grace period, your subscription status is marked as "past due" but access continues.
  • If payment is not resolved, the subscription is cancelled and your school reverts to the free tier.
  • Student data is preserved. Resubscribing restores access.

5. Exceptions

We may grant refunds outside the standard policy in exceptional circumstances, including:

  • Extended service outages (24+ hours) affecting your school
  • Billing errors or duplicate charges
  • Documented technical issues preventing use of the service

Contact support@brainsheist.com to discuss.

6. Paddle as Merchant of Record

Paddle.com Market Limited is the Merchant of Record for all Brains Heist subscriptions. This means:

  • Paddle handles all payment processing, invoicing, and tax collection.
  • Your invoice will show Paddle as the seller.
  • Refunds are processed by Paddle to your original payment method.
  • Paddle's Buyer Terms also apply to your purchase.

7. Contact

For billing questions or refund requests:

  • Email: support@brainsheist.com
  • Response time: Within 1 business day for billing issues
  • See our Contact page for more ways to reach us.
