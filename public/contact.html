Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to School Admin Portal → Billing → Manage Subscription, or email support@brainsheist.com. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.
Can I get a refund?
We offer a 14-day refund window from your initial purchase. Auto-renewal refunds are available within 48 hours. Full details in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
How do I switch plans?
School admins can upgrade or downgrade in the Billing tab. Upgrades are instant with prorated pricing; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Who processes my payment?
Paddle is our Merchant of Record. They handle payment processing, invoicing, and tax. Your invoice will show Paddle as the seller.
Is student data safe?
Yes. We use Supabase with Row-Level Security, encrypted database connections, and never share student data with third parties for marketing. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Do you support schools outside the UK?
Yes — Brains Heist is available worldwide. Paddle handles multi-currency billing and local tax compliance so you're covered regardless of location.
