Delete your Brains Heist account

Brains Heist LLC · Last updated: November 5, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Brains Heist account and its data at any time. This applies to accounts used on brainsheist.com and in the Brains Heist app for Android and iPhone.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@brainsheist.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Brains Heist account".
  2. Tell us your username or the name on the account, so we can find it.
  3. We confirm by email, then delete the account within 30 days.

School students: if your account was created by your school, you can also ask your teacher or school administrator. School administrators can delete student accounts directly from the School Admin portal.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, email address, avatar, school and class links.
  • Your learning data: game progress, XP, coins, achievements, test answers, scores, IELTS course progress and streaks.
  • Your sign-in details, including the link to "Sign in with Google".

What we may keep

  • Billing records (for example, IELTS Prime purchases): kept for up to 7 years because the law requires it. They do not include your learning data.
  • Anonymous statistics that can no longer identify you, such as total numbers of completed lessons.
  • Security logs: deleted within 90 days.

Purchases made in the app through Google Play or the Apple App Store are managed by those stores. Deleting your Brains Heist account does not cancel a store subscription, so please cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple ID settings as well.

Questions

Email support@brainsheist.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
