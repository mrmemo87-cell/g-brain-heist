Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

Summary: Brains Heist is a school-facing educational platform. We collect minimal personal data, never sell it, and store it securely on Supabase. Payments are handled by Paddle — we never see or store payment card details.

1. Who We Are

Brains Heist ("we", "us", "our") operates the gamified learning platform at brainsheist.com. For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@brainsheist.com.

2.3 Payment Data

All payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited (our Merchant of Record). We never collect, store, or have access to credit card numbers, bank details, or other payment instruments. Paddle handles all payment data in accordance with their Privacy Policy and PCI-DSS compliance.

We only receive from Paddle: subscription status, plan type, billing period dates, and customer ID — no payment card details.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide the Service: Authenticate users, deliver game content, track progress, generate reports.
  • Billing: Manage subscription status and entitlements (via Paddle).
  • Security: Detect abuse, enforce bans, prevent unauthorized access.
  • Improvement: Aggregate, anonymized analytics to improve features. We do not profile individual students for advertising.
  • Communication: Service announcements, billing receipts, and password resets. We do not send marketing emails unless explicitly opted in.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with:

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or social media platforms.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security (RLS) enforced — users can only access data they are authorized to see.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
  • Access to production databases is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We conduct periodic security audits and follow secure development practices.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained as long as the account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Personal data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Aggregate/anonymized data may be retained indefinitely.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years to comply with financial record-keeping requirements.
  • Webhook event logs: Raw payloads are retained for 90 days for debugging, then purged.

7. Children's Privacy

Brains Heist is designed for use in schools. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without the involvement of their school. Schools are responsible for obtaining any required parental/guardian consent.

We follow applicable children's data protection best practices, including COPPA (US) and age-appropriate design principles, where applicable to school-administered services.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@brainsheist.com. We will respond within 30 days.

School administrators can delete student accounts and associated data directly from the School Admin portal.

9. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies:

  • Authentication cookie (Supabase session token) — essential, not optional.
  • Local storage for UI preferences (light mode, cached data) — essential.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies.

10. International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States (Supabase, Vercel) and the European Union (Paddle). Where data is transferred across jurisdictions, we use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to school administrators at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact

For privacy concerns:
