About Brains Heist

Where school feels like a game.

Who we are

Brains Heist is an education app made by Brains Heist LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, USA. It was started by a teacher and is used by schools and students in Central Asia.

What Brains Heist does

🎮

Learn by playing

Short daily missions in English and maths, with rewards, streaks and friendly battles.

🏫

For schools

Class competitions, assignments, Cambridge-style practice tests and progress reports for teachers and school leaders.

🔥

Brains Heist IELTS

A self-paced IELTS course: short lessons, real practice in all four papers and instant feedback, with Blaze the streak flame.

Who it is for

Students aged 13 and over, their teachers and their schools. Students usually join with a code from their school; IELTS learners can also sign up on their own.
