Common questions

I can't sign in
Check that you are using the same email or username your school gave you. If you forgot your password, ask your teacher or school admin to reset it, or email us from the address on your account.
How do I join my school or class?
Ask your teacher for your school's invite code and enter it when you sign up. If the code doesn't work, your teacher or school admin can check it, or email us.
How do I manage or cancel IELTS Prime?
If you bought IELTS Prime in the Android app, manage or cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. If you bought it in the iPhone app, use Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For anything else about a payment, email support@brainsheist.com.
How do I delete my account?
Follow the steps on our account deletion page. We confirm by email and delete the account and its data within 30 days.
How is my data protected?
Read our Privacy Policy. For privacy or data requests, email privacy@brainsheist.com.
I'm a school and want Brains Heist
Email sales@brainsheist.com and we'll set up your school.
