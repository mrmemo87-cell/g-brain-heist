# What If You Could See the Future Through the Mirror?

Social-experiment What-If, told from your own (embarrassing) point of view. Target ~6 min.
N = narrator. Other names are character voices.

## Visual rules (director's notes)
- **The hook device:** a mirror shows you exactly 24 hours from now, so real-you and the reflection never match. Every mirror has a camcorder-style label in the corner: "● TOMORROW · 7:02 AM".
- **Decisions update the future live:** whenever you decide something, the glass glitches (cyan/magenta slices + flash + click) and the reflection changes.
- **Over-the-shoulder framing:** real-you is shown from behind in the foreground, tomorrow-you faces the camera in the glass, so the viewer stands where you stand.
- **Camera language:** slow push-in only on emotional lines; snap-zoom + shake on punchlines/shocks; whip-pan (with motion blur) between the phone and the mirror; extreme close-up for reveals; inserts (phone screens, hands) to keep the rhythm under 3 s per change in fast sections.
- **First 2 seconds:** open on an extreme close-up of tomorrow-you crying, then pull back to reveal calm today-you brushing their teeth.
