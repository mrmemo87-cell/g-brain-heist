## Connective wording candidates

### english-connective-repair-20261026-31

Choose a connective that reinforces the first statement: The new instructions improved motivation; ____ , students completed more practice tasks after the instructions were introduced.

A. however
B. indeed
C. meanwhile
D. otherwise

**Key: B — indeed**

Replace the untraceable Author (2019) attribution with a self-contained classroom context.

### english-connective-repair-20261026-48

Choose a phrase that introduces a cautious inference: Across repeated trials, the measured results contradicted the prediction; ____ the proposed explanation may be wrong.

A. regarding
B. in the meantime
C. this suggests that
D. nevertheless

**Key: C — this suggests that**

Contradictory results support an inference; merely weak evidence does not establish that a hypothesis is wrong.

### english-connective-repair-20261026-108

Choose a connective that restates the first idea: The system is unaffordable for most students; ____ , it costs more than they can pay.

A. nevertheless
B. meanwhile
C. otherwise
D. in other words

**Key: D — in other words**

Use the natural explicit restatement marker in other words instead of the less natural in other terms.

### english-connective-repair-20261026-117

Choose a connective that adds a confirming detail: The plan is inefficient; ____ , it repeats every check three times.

A. in fact
B. however
C. meanwhile
D. otherwise

**Key: A — in fact**

The second clause confirms inefficiency; rather misleadingly suggests correction or an alternative.
